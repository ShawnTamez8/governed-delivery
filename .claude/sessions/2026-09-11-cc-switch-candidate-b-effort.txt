Material scope clarifications before implementation:
- Matching env does not prove authentication, model entitlement, quota, or
  provider reachability. The review's suggestion to stop conceding those
  limitations is too strong for a version-only probe. Keep the limitations.
- A short unsalted hash of a credential is not safe secret redaction.
  Credential-like variables should expose presence only. Fingerprint an
  explicit non-secret allowlist; never dump values or credential-store
  contents. Whole-file hashes are change observations, not parsed credentials.
- Environment presence does not mean child delivery: current
  ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL, ANTHROPIC_AUTH_TOKEN, and OPENAI_BASE_URL are excluded.
  User-file reachability is a different route and needs separate reporting.
- Reachable configuration files are not proof that the invoked version,
  flags, or precedence rules actually use those files. Bound the initial
  inventory to documented Claude user-config locations and report unknown
  precedence explicitly. Do not implement universal harness discovery.
- An absolute path observed by doctor is not a binary pin for a later
  dispatch. Working directories, PATH, home configuration, and binaries can
  change after observation. Full freezing or drift enforcement is not B.
- Doctor observations are current and not stored as historical agent/run
  telemetry. Candidate B does not provide progress events, token counters,
  cost recalculation, or dashboard transport/history.
